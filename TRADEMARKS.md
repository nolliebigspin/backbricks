# Trademark policy

The code of Corbel is open source. The **name "Corbel" and the Corbel logo** are not covered by the open-source licenses; they identify the official project and its managed cloud. This policy explains how you may use them. Registration of the trademark is in progress.

## You may, without asking

- Refer to the project by name in articles, talks, tutorials, docs and comparisons.
- Say that your product, plugin or integration "works with Corbel" or is "compatible with Corbel", as long as it's accurate and doesn't suggest endorsement.
- Use the name for unmodified self-hosted deployments of the official releases, including for your clients.
- Keep the name in forks while they're used internally or for development.

## Please ask first

- Using the name or logo in a product, company or domain name.
- Merchandise or event names.

## You may not

- Offer a hosted or commercial service called "Corbel" (or a confusingly similar name) based on a modified version, or imply that it's the official Corbel cloud.
- Distribute a modified version publicly under the name "Corbel". Rename your fork and state that it's based on Corbel.
- Use the logo in a way that suggests endorsement or official affiliation.

Questions: open a discussion on GitHub or contact the maintainers.

*This policy is not legal advice and will be reviewed before the `v1.0.0` release.*
